Our story

more than just coffee

Aurelia was born from a love of quiet mornings, hand-poured espresso, and the smell of butter croissants. We source our beans from small farms in Colombia and Ethiopia, roast them locally, and brew each cup with intention.

Our space is designed for lingering — warm wood, soft light, and a soundtrack of gentle jazz. Whether you’re here to work, meet a friend, or simply breathe, we’re glad you’re here.

— The Aurelia family
